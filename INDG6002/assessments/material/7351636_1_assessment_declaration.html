Assessment declaration

Original Blackboard item · _7351636_1

By submitting assessments for grading, you agree that:

  • you have read and understood the University’s academic integrity rules.
  • the work you submit is your own and properly referenced, and it has not been submitted in another unit.
  • you have followed the university guidelines for GenAI use and the GenAI use conditions outlined in the assessment brief and/or by the unit assessor.
  • you understand the consequences of academic misconduct.
  • your work may be checked for authenticity, and making a false declaration is a serious breach of academic integrity.
