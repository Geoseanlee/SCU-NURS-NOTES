INDG6002 EXAMPLE STRUCTURE ASSESSMENT 1

Students MAY USE the following structure, including the Headings:



Introduction (Approx. 100 words)

   •   Introduce UNDRIP Article 3.

   •   Outline the main points of your response.

Health and Wellbeing (Approx. 300 words)

   •   Examine the key concepts of health and wellbeing from Australian Indigenous
       perspectives.

   •   Reflect on why self-determination is important for Australian Indigenous health and
       wellbeing.

   •   Critically evaluate one cultural determinant which shapes Australian Indigenous health
       and wellbeing.

Community-accepted communication styles (Approx. 300 words)

Critically analyse the role of community-accepted communication styles in supporting
Australian Indigenous health and wellbeing.



Colonisation and Health (Approx. 200 words)

   •   Critically reflect on the enduring effects of colonisation on Australian Indigenous health
       and wellbeing.

Conclusion (Approx. 100 words)

   •   Summarise your key points. NO new information
