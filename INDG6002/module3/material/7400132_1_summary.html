Summary

Original Blackboard item · _7400132_1

This module as defined and discussed the social and cultural determinants of health and why they so integrally connect to health outcomes for Indigenous Australian Peoples. The strong parallels between all Indigenous Nations are evidenced in the description of the SDoH from a Canadian Ktunaxa woman. We urged you to reflect on what culture means to you; and, how your own culture may impact members of other cultural groups. We have touched on the topic area of epigenetics and explored how focusing on the cultural determinants can provide a framework to improve health outcomes for Indigenous Australian Peoples. In Module 4 we focus on the theoretical framework of Cultural Safety, its origins; how practicing cultural safety can enhance your ability to work respectfully with culturally diverse peoples.
