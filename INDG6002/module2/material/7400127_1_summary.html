Original Blackboard item · _7400127_1

This module has provided a brief overview of historical and ongoing events and issues that remain as barriers to accessible and acceptable health and social services for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Peoples. The deliberate omission of the truth about the brutality of colonisation, as exemplared by the taking of this continent by the British using terra nullius is described. The immediate and transgenerational trauma inflicted on Indigenous families and communities from the Stolen Generations has been touched upon. Institutional and systemic racism is defined and detailed.
