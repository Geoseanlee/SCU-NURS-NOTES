This week’s tutorial

Original Blackboard item · _7400125_1

Historical and contemporary barriers to equitable health and social support for Indigenous Australian Peoples will be discussed and considered. The focus will be on disconnection from Country, Culture & Family as drivers of the ‘gap’ in relation to Indigenous Peoples' health and wellbeing. Blatant and covert forms of racism will be discussed in relation to institutional racism and how you as individual practitioners can respond. This week we will introduce the concept of responding to discrimination and racism within services and decolonising your professional practice as ‘everybody’s business’.
