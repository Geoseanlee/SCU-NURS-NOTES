## History is a contemporary determinant

The module connects invasion, dispossession, forced separation and racialised policy with current health and service access. Do not describe colonisation as a finished event or explain present inequities only through individual behaviour.

## Terra nullius and the Mabo decision

The course examines how terra nullius denied recognition of existing peoples’ rights and how the 1992 Mabo decision rejected that doctrine in Australian common law. Distinguish this legal milestone from a claim that its consequences or dispossession were thereby resolved.

## The Stolen Generations and trauma

Forced child removal disrupted family, language, identity, culture and Country. Intergenerational trauma describes continuing consequences across families and generations; it is not a statement that every individual has the same experience. Read the Bringing Them Home material and Healing Foundation resources with attention to survivors’ voices.

## Racism in services

Interpersonal racism concerns interactions; institutional and systemic racism also operate through routine rules, resource allocation and assumptions about acceptable care. An organisation may reproduce inequity without an openly racist statement by a single worker.

## Practice and revision

Explain why reluctance to attend a mainstream service should not automatically be labelled non-compliance. Trace a pathway from historical policy to present service conditions and health consequences. Identify a response at practitioner, organisational and policy levels. Respect the module’s trigger warning and use support resources when needed.

## Build an explanatory chain

| Level | Question to ask | Example of a response to examine |
|---|---|---|
| Historical policy | What happened to Country, family, language, resources and authority? | Read the original history and survivor accounts rather than treating colonisation as background scenery |
| Present structures | How do funding, location, eligibility rules and institutional racism shape access now? | Examine organisational policy and resource allocation |
| Service encounter | Whose account is trusted, and can the person exercise meaningful consent and choice? | Change communication and respond to racism instead of labelling reluctance as non-compliance |
| Accountability | Who evaluates whether care improved? | Include community-defined outcomes, not only the institution’s activity totals |
