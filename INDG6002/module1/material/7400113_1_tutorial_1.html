Discussion on key concepts you have learnt that link to your Assessment Tasks. Together we will discuss Indigenous ways of knowing, being and doing in health. Through class discussions you have the opportunity to deepen your understanding of the Social and Emotional Well-being framework, Yarning and Storytelling that culturally shape communication styles. Reflecting on your own worldview is a crucial skill for working with Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Peoples in a culturally safe and respectful way.

The Unit structure and Assessment Tasks will be explained in the Week 1 Workshop (on-line). You will have the opportunity to ask questions about Unit Content and Assessement Tasks at weekly Workshops, so bring your questions along. As the Workshops will be recorded, your queries can also assist your peers who might ask similar questions.

Reflection

Reflecting on these, how has your understanding been challenged/ validated in relation to working with Indigenous Australian Peoples.

Question

What do you expect to learn from this unit that can expand your understanding of how Indigenous Australian peoples’ health and well-being has been impacted by the dominance of the western biomedical model? Post your response to the discussion board.
