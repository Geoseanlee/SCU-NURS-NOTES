Summary

Source: INDG6002-2026-T5 Module 1, item 13_summary.html (SCU Blackboard, learn.scu.edu.au)

This module has introduced you to Australian Indigenous Peoples' concepts and worldviews different approaches relating to health and well-being in contrast with western biomedical health concepts. The Australian Indigenous wholistic framework of Social and Emotional Wellbeing (SEWB) was considered. The culturally informed communications styles Yarning, Clinical yarning, Storytelling and Dadirri deep listening skills were discussed.
