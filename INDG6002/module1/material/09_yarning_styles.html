Podcast on Yarning style – Rachel Lynwood

Podcast 1:

indg6002_m1_Rachel-Lynwood-Podcast-1.m4a


Time stamp: 0.00 to 27:21

Gnibi academic and Bundjalung woman Rachel Lynwood delivers an informal Yarning style Podcast about Social and Emotional Wellbeing and Indigenous worldviews on health. Rachel suggests the lack of:

  • Recognition of trauma and
  • Prioritisation of SEWB by mainstream health organisations

contributes to high rates of suicide within Indigenous communities. The reading below is referred to in the podcast.
