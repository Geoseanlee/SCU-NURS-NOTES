How might decolonising your practice look and feel?

Original Blackboard item · _7400141_1

Obviously, in order to decolonise your practice, you need to first decolonise your own lens on working with Indigenous Peoples. In Module 4 we explored cultural safety as the appropriate and comprehensive framework to achieve this. Cultural safety is a tool to dismantle and unsettle the dominant ways of thinking about health and social care. A decolonising of your practice inevitably includes the examination of the omnipresent existence of race that exists in human relationships (Walter & Baltra-Ulloa, 2019).

Never under-estimate the power and privilege that comes with being a university educated professional and a member of the dominant culture in this country. Decolonising your practice requires an ability to acknowledge this, and how you work towards reclaiming trust and address the fear, loss of identity, loss of power and destruction of culture and families experienced by Indigenous Peoples since the arrival of the colonisers.

"Decolonisation is not a simple act, it is a journey that requires more than simple words and cannot be achieved in a short time frame.”

(Green & Bennett, 2018 p. 262)

This aligns with Rix et al. (2023, p. 6), who caution that “Positive rhetoric in policies and guidelines in no way guarantees a shift in practice”

Taken together, these perspectives highlight the gap that often exists between intention and transformation, reinforcing that decolonising practice demands long‑term, relational, and structural change rather than short‑term or performative responses.

Complete the readings below via MyReadings:

Green, S., & Bennett, B. (2018). Wayanha: A Decolonised Social Work. Australian Social Work, 71(3), 261–264. doi:10.1080/0312407X.2018.1469654

This paper, written by Indigenous Australian social workers, provides an in-depth exploration and discussion of decolonisation in the context of working with Indigenous Australian Peoples, where Aboriginality is viewed through a deficit and problematising lens since Australia was invaded based on Terra Nullius.
