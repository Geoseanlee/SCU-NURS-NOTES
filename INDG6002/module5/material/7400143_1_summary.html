This Module has defined decolonisation and provided rationale and context for decolonising your perspective on working with Indigenous Peoples as clients in health and social care settings. Your understanding of decolonising professional practice has been expanded by a short presentation/lecture from a North American Indigenous scholar and social worker. The ‘problematising’ of Indigenous People by mainstream media organisations remains a barrier to decolonising of practice for health and social care professionals. Your readings and videos this week from Indigenous scholars in Australia help to demystify what a decolonised way of thinking and working with Indigenous Peoples across all services provision settings may look like.

In your final Module for this unit, we will look at how the principles of Primary Healthcare and Health Promotion align and resonate with Indigenous Peoples health and ways of knowing, being and doing. These settings are highly relevant to health and social care professionals, as this is where many of you are likely to encounter and work with Indigenous Peoples.
