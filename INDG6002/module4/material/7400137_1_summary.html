Summary

Original Blackboard item · _7400137_1

In this Module we defined and described cultural safety, (designed by an Indigenous nurse for Indigenous Peoples), as the only framework for working with cultural diverse peoples. The core principles have been presented and discussed, by way of readings and videos. A 'real-life scenario’ aimed at deeper understanding of cultural differences may help to inform your Assessment Task 2.

Next week we will focus on synthesising your learning across Modules 1 to 4 to provide you with knowledge and skills for reflecting on decolonising your own practices when working with Indigenous clients.
