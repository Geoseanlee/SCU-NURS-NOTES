Module overview

Original Blackboard item · _7400144_1

Your final Module this week provides information on the strong connections between Indigenous health and wellbeing perspectives and the principles of Primary Health Care and Health Promotion. The origins and principles of Indigenous health frameworks are discussed by way of two key historical documents.  Aboriginal Health Workers' roles are described. The Aboriginal Community Controlled Health sector’s role as a world leader in early Primary Health Care is acknowledged.

Your final reading this week confirms why Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Peoples' Country, family and culture are profoundly related to optimal health and wellbeing. Recognising and comprehending the circular nature and interconnection of the different aspects of your learning journey in this Unit is important for holding new perspectives.

Module purpose

By the end of this module you will be able to:

  • define and discuss Primary Health Care and Health Promotion and principles
  • understand how and why these models of health resonate so strongly with Indigenous worldviews on health and wellbeing
  • describe the work and roles of Aboriginal Health Workers and Aboriginal Community Controlled Health Organisations
  • comprehend the interconnected learning and topics from Modules 1–6 of this unit.
