In this final module we have presented the principles of Primary Health Care and Health Promotion in the context of mirroring Indigenous perspectives on health and wellbeing. Primary health care’s focus on accessible and acceptable health services that are culturally safe and target the needs of a particular community, reflect the health and social needs of Indigenous Peoples. The principles of Health Promotion such as empowerment, strengthening community action, and reorienting health services originated from the early Aboriginal Community Controlled Health sector in Redfern and beyond. The crucial role of Aboriginal Health Workers within their communities is highlighted, along with the leading role that Aboriginal Community Controlled Health Organisations play in leading the way for PHC and HP initiatives aimed at delivering health equity for vulnerable and disadvantaged people in Australia and in other First World nations.
