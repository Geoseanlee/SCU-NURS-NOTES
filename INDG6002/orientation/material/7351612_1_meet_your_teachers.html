Dr Darlene Rotumah

PhD | MIndgSt | BA (Welfare Studies)

Darlene is a Bundjalung woman with extensive experience in the health sector. She is dedicated to strengthening students’ understanding of how to build respectful, culturally informed, and trusting relationships with Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Peoples across health and social service contexts. Drawing on Indigenous knowledge systems, cultural practices, and community‑centred approaches to learning, she supports students to recognise the depth, cultural realities, and enduring cultural presence of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander worldviews. Her teaching encourages future practitioners to engage with Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Peoples in ways that honour cultural authority, promote self‑determination, and uphold culturally safe care.

Dr Ros Sawtell (she/her)

Lecturer

Workshops & Face-to-Face classes

Ros is a Koori woman dedicated to strengthening health and education through truth-telling, cultural knowledge, and respect. With a career grounded in community, Ros focuses on supporting students to deeply understand the worldviews, strengths, resilience, and histories of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples. She brings a relational, strengths-based approach to teaching and advocacy, working to ensure future health professionals are equipped to practise with cultural humility, integrity, and accountability.

Dr Coral Jeffries

PhD | MSc | BHSc | BSc

Online Tutor

Coral is an educator committed to supporting students to develop a thoughtful and respectful understanding of the complex relationships between health and Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Peoples. Her teaching emphasises cultural safety, reflexivity, and the importance of recognising the historical, social, and structural factors that shape health experiences and outcomes. She encourages students to engage with Indigenous perspectives with humility, curiosity, and respect, and to consider their responsibilities as future practitioners working within diverse communities.
