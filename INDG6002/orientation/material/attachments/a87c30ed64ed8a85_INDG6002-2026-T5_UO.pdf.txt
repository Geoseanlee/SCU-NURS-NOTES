About this unit
Raises understanding of Indigenous health issues including: Indigenous concepts and
frameworks for health; the negative impact of colonisation on health and wellbeing; cultural
safety; and reflection on how to decolonise professional practice. Awareness of best-practice
Indigenous health care strategies will be elevated through discussion, critical analysis, and
service planning exercises.



Modules
Module 1 – Contrasting Aboriginal and western Health Perspectives

Module 2 – Colonisation and Health

Module 3 – Social and cultural determinants of health

Module 4 – Cultural Safety

Module 5 – Decolonising your Practice

Module 6 – Working in Indigenous communities

Unit learning outcomes
As a graduate of Southern Cross University, you will have developed skills, values and attitudes
that are essential for gaining employment and advancing lifelong learning. The University
refers to these as graduate attributes.



 Learning outcomes for this unit

      1. synthesise key concepts of health and wellbeing as determined by Aboriginal
         people

      2. critically analyse key stages of the colonisation process and its relationship to the
         contemporary health status of Australian Indigenous people

      3. critically analyse the relationship between socio-economic factors and the
         contemporary health status of Australian Indigenous people

      4. reflect on and synthesise how primary health care and/or health promotion
         enhances the wellbeing of Indigenous people and role of the health professional as
         an agent of social change
