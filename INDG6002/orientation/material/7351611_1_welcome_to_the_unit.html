Unit overview

Raises understanding of Indigenous health issues including: Indigenous concepts and frameworks for health; the negative impact of colonisation on health and wellbeing; cultural safety; and reflection on how to decolonise professional practice. Awareness of best-practice Indigenous health care strategies will be elevated through discussion, critical analysis, and service planning exercises.

Unit Learning Outcomes (ULOs)

By the end of this unit, you will be able to:

  • ULO 1 Synthesise key concepts of health and wellbeing as determined by Aboriginal People
  • ULO 2 critically analyse key stages of the colonisation process and its relationship to the contemporary health status of Australian Indigenous people
  • ULO 3 critically analyse the relationship between socio-economic factors and the contemporary health status of Australian Indigenous people
  • ULO 4 reflect on and synthesise how primary health care and/or health promotion enhances the wellbeing of Indigenous people and role of the health professional as an agent of social change
