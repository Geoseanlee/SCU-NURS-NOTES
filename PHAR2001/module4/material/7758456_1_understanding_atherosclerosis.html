Introduction to Atherosclerosis

'Atherosclerosis' is a strange word,what does it mean? Well, 'sclero' comes from the Greek sklērós, meaning “hard.” Atherosclerosis therefore literally means hardening of the arteries.

It is a chronic inflammatory condition that results in fibrosis, scarring and narrowing of the arteries. But more importantly, atherosclerosis is the underlying cause of about 50% of all deaths in westernized society. Therefore we should take the time to learn as much about the disorder as possible because many of our therapeutic agents will target the risk factors of the disorder that allow the problem to progress.
