Introduction to lipids

Not all lipids are ‘bad’. They perform many functions both within the body, as they act as an energy reserve, regulate hormones, transmit nerve impulses, cushion vital organs, and transport fat-soluble nutrients. They are also important in the structure of cell membranes and hormones. This lesson helps you understand more about when it may be time to treat the lipid levels in the body.
