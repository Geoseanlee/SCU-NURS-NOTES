Overview of Thiazide diuretics

The Thiazide and thiazide like diuretics are the most widely used diuretics. They are often combined with other diuretics to increase the efficacy and decrease the side effects. For instance, thiazide diuretics waste potassium. But if a small amount of thiazide diuretics is added along with a potassium sparing diuretic or to ACE inhibitors, or angiotensin receptor blockers, the potassium levels often normalise and the diuresis increases. Find more about the thiazide diuretics like hydrochlorothiazide and the thiazide like diuretics like metolazone in this lesson.
