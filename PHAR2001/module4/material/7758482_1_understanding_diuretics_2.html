The role of diuretics in heart failure and hypertension

The main indications for the use of diuretics are congestive heart failure and hypertension. In CHF, there is additional fluid being forced into the interstitial spaces due to increased back pressure. Relieving some of that fluid is beneficial as the heart can work more efficiently. In hypertension, recall that blood pressure is partly dependent on the amount of fluid, and as such, a reduction of fluid is likely to reduce BP - not dramatically, but reductions of 6-12 mmHg are common. Find out more of the foundational information about diuretics in this lesson.
