The role of platelets in haemostasis and drug targeting

Platelets are invaluable in the process of haemostasis, and as such, most of our antiplatelet drugs will not disable the platelet altogether, but rather just take away one of the platelet's 'tools'. In order to understand our antiplatelet drugs, the most important thing to remember is that platelets are not cells, and as such, they do not contain the DNA to synthesise more 'tools'. So when we take away one of the platelet's tools that allows for optimal aggregation, that 'tool' will be disabled for the entire life of the platelet, which is 9 days.
