## ppt/slides/slide17.xml
Patients commencing diuretics 
Care must be taken to avoid fluid rebound. 
Some patients decrease water consumption when they commence diuretics to decrease trips to the bathroom
If water is decreased at the same time as diuretic is commenced, rebound oedema will result!

## ppt/slides/slide20.xml
Thiazide diuretics (hydrochlorothiazide)
Thiazide like diuretic (metolazone) 
Inhibit sodium reabsorption from the distal tubule
Wastes potassium as well 
Used for hypertension, congestive heart failure, nephrolithiasis (kidney stones) and diabetes insipidus
Thiazide diuretics compete with the secretion of uric acid so they are contraindicated in people with gout
Several drugs use hydrochlorothiazide in combination – a little bit increases the action of other
Hydrochlorothiazide 
‘thiazide” drugs

## ppt/slides/slide26.xml
Loop diuretics 
Furosemide (Lasix) and other loop diuretics work on the ascending loop of Henle 
They indirectly block active transportation of sodium, potassium and chloride into the interstitial space where they would normally re-enter circulation
Sodium rich urine 
Work in the ascending loop of 
Henle

## ppt/slides/slide31.xml
Potassium-sparing diuretics
Spironolactone
Blocks aldosterone at the distal tubule
Used for any aldosterone excess states. 
Toxicity includes hyperkalaemia (raised k+ potassium levels) 
Blocks androgen as well (dysmenorrhoea in women, breast development in males)

## ppt/slides/slide32.xml
Potassium-sparing diuretics
Potassium-sparing diuretics
Spironolactone (Aldactone): Aldosterone antagonist
Amiloride: Blocks sodium channels
Spironolactone  was at one time thought to be important for all patients with hypertension but it causes hyperkalaemia
Some say it should not be used with ACE inhibitors, but some people can tolerate it

## ppt/slides/slide38.xml
ADH and aldosterone
ACE inhibitors act as diuretics by indirectly decreasing the ADH and aldosterone secretion
Despite this, ACE inhibitors are not considered diuretics (they are in a class of their own and have diuretic actions)
Some of the other diuretics act on 
ADH the water saver and 
aldosterone the salt saver
