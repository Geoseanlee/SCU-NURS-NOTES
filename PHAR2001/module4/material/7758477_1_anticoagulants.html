Pharmacological regulation of coagulation

The second main step in the stoppage of blood (haemo- stasis) is the clotting cascade. Have you ever baited a mouse with mouse and rat bait and wondered how they die? Well the bait is an anticoagulant that is used in warfarin. As you can understand it is very important to keep the dose of warfarin in the correct range, because too high a dose could result in haemorrhages which too little can result in excessive clotting. While there are newer rat baits and newer anticoagulants available, you can understand how important these medications are.
