The thiazide diuretics like hydrochlorothiazide are one of the first line treatments for hypertension, as a small amount of that drug can be given to increase the blood pressure lowering effects of other antihypertensive agents

For treating hypertension, one should start out with small dose of one of the first line treatments for hypertension and then keep increasing the dose if it is not effective.

It is not uncommon for a client to be on all three first line treatments for hypertension (an ACE Inhibitor or ARB, a thiazide diuretic, AND a calcium channel blocker) all at the same time, as these all work by different mechanisms and as such can add additional antihypertensive actions.

Most diuretics work by directly pulling water out of the system.
