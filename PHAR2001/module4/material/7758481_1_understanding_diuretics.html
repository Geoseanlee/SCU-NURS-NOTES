The functional unit in the kidney is the nephron, and some of the cells in the nephron contain more energy dependent pumps than any other cells in the human body.

Increasing the diameter of the afferent arteriole and/or decreasing the diameter of the efferent arteriole will have the ultimate effect of increasing pressure in the glomerulus and an increase in glomerular filtration rate.

‘Tubular reabsorption’ is a term that is often misunderstood by students as it actually means that the substance is being reabsorbed out of the tubule and into the peritubular capillaries, where it can re-enter systemic circulation

Glucose and most proteins do not enter the tubules because those are substances that we want to keep.
