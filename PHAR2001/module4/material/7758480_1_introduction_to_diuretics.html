           Thiazide diuretics (hydrochlorothiazide)
             Thiazide like diuretic (metolazone)
      • Inhibit sodium reabsorption from the distal tubule
      • Wastes potassium as well
      • Used for hypertension, congestive heart failure,
        nephrolithiasis (kidney stones) and diabetes insipidus
      • Thiazide diuretics compete with the secretion of uric acid so
        they are contraindicated in people with gout

      • Several drugs use hydrochlorothiazide in combination – a
        little bit increases the action of other
          Hydrochlorothiazide
          ‘thiazide” drugs

                   Potassium-sparing diuretics

                            Spironolactone
     • Blocks aldosterone at the distal tubule
     • Used for any aldosterone excess states.
     • Toxicity includes hyperkalaemia (raised k+ potassium levels)
     • Blocks androgen as well (dysmenorrhoea in women, breast
       development in males)




31




                   Potassium-sparing diuretics
     • Potassium-sparing diuretics
        • Spironolactone (Aldactone): Aldosterone antagonist
        • Amiloride: Blocks sodium channels

     • Spironolactone was at one time thought to be important for all
       patients with hypertension but it causes hyperkalaemia
     • Some say it should not be used with ACE inhibitors, but some
       people can tolerate it

34




                           Osmotic diuretics

      • Non-reabsorbable solute pulls water in
      • Used to maintain urine volume and reduce intracranial
        and intraocular pressure

      • Initially expands fluid volume (bad for CHF)
      • Then high sodium because sodium is not following
      • Produces hyperkalaemia

                                   ADH and aldosterone

       • ACE inhibitors act as diuretics by indirectly decreasing
         the ADH and aldosterone secretion
       • Despite this, ACE inhibitors are not considered
         diuretics (they are in a class of their own and have
         diuretic actions)
       • Some of the other diuretics act on
          • ADH the water saver and
          • aldosterone the salt saver
