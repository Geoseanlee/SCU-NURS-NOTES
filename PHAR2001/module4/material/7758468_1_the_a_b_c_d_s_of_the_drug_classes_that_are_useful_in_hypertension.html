Hypertension Pharmacology made simple: The ABCD framework

When we're thinking about the drugs that are used to treat hypertension, one of the ways in which we can easily remember those drugs is to simply remember the first four letters of the alphabet. We have spoken about a few of these medications already, and the next few lessons will bring it all together for you. Remember, A, B, C and D:

  • Alpha 1 blockers (…azosin drugs)
  • Alpha 2 agonists (yes, agonists, 
  • Angiotensin converting enzyme (ACE) inhibitors
  • Angiotensin 2 receptor blockers (ARBS; …sartan)
  • Beta blockers
  • Calcium channel blockers (Dihydropyridines)
  • Diuretics – Especially Hydrochlorothiazide or thiazide diuretics

So, with respect to the alpha blockers, they are adrenergic antagonists that selectively affect the peripheral blood vessels.

The drugs of the renin cascade, being the angiotensin converting enzyme (ace inhibitors) and angiotensin 2 receptor blockers (ARBS),

The beta blockers that block the adrenaline binding to the heart

The calcium channel blockers that do not allow the calcium into the cells of the heart (calcium is needed for contraction)

And the diuretics, which we will discuss in the last lesson of this module.
