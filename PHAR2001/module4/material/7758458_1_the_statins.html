Given the importance of lipids in the process of atherosclerosis, we have several types of medications that aim to normalise the lipid levels. In this lesson, we explore the Hmg CoA reductase inhibitors, or more commonly referred to as the statins because all of the members of that drug class end in the suffix ...STATIN.
