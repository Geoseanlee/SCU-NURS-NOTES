Why beta blockers are no longer first-line therapy for hypertension

Have you ever wondered how long it takes between the time that scientists discover that a drug class is no longer the first-line therapy for a certain disorder and the time that doctors stop prescribing it for that disorder? Well, it has been over ten years since better alternatives for hypertension have been found, and atenolol and other beta blockers are still the first choices for many doctors.

Lesson learnt: Try to keep up with the most recent information about the first-line therapies, because they do change, and quite frequently. This lesson explains why the beta blockers are no longer #1.
