Dyslipidaemia and HDL

Recall that we use the term dyslipidaemia for individuals who have low levels of HDL, the good protein, because that is a risk factor for developing atherosclerosis. HDL is the ‘bus’ that carries cholesterol from the tissues back to the liver, so a low amount of the HDL will yield high amounts near the blood vessels. Nicotinic acid raises the HDL levels, but if that is not able to be tolerated, fenofibrate or other fibrates can be helpful. It is one of the few times that you will still see those medications still used.
