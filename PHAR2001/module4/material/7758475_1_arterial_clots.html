From haemostasis to thrombosis: mechanisms and consequences

Haemostasis is the process of stopping bleeding. We use it every day to repair small bleeds, and it is vital to our survival. But our lifestyle has lead to us being predisposed to this process occurring in absence of a break in the blood vessel walls. In that case, a thrombus develops. Thrombi can occlude the site in which it develops or they can break off from the original site of formation to a blood vessel downstream. In order to combat the development of thrombi, we target the same process in which they develop, the platelet plug, the clotting cascade and the natural dissolving of the clot. Engage in this lesson to understand why our medications are effective.
