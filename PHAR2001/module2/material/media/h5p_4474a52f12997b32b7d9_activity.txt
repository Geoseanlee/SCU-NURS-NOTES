The poisonous, pretty mushrooms that were seen in this lesson contain the alkaloid MUSCARINE, which binds to the acetylcholine MUSCARINIC receptors in the PNS, and acts like an agonist. (it acts like acetylcholine)  As such they would cause which of the following?

Untitled Multiple Choice

true

Direct acting cholinergic drugs of the PNS act like acetylcholine on the muscarinic receptors. Indirect acting cholinergic drugs inhibit the enzyme acetylcholinesterase, that breaks down acetylcholine in the synaptic cleft, allowing the actual nerurotransmitter acetylcholine to remain in the synaptic cleft longer.
