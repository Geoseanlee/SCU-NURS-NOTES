Overview of the alpha 1 receptor

The alpha 1 receptor is found on peripheral blood vessels and the prostate. If you think about it, you will probably guess that they would be potentially useful in benign prostatic hyperplasia and hypertension. We talk about these in a couple spots in the unit, so this lesson is less than 2 minutes long.
