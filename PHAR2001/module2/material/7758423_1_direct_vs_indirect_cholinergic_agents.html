Differences between direct and indirect cholinergic agents

For the most part, our ANS medications are receptor agonists and antagonists. But one notable exception is the indirect acting cholinergic agents that are enzymatic inhibitors. They act on and inhibit the enzyme that degrades acetylcholine. Let's take a look at the differences between direct and indirect cholinergic agents.
