Introduction to selective alpha agonists

The alpha-adrenergic agents will bind to and act like adrenaline on the peripheral blood vessels, the radial muscles of the eyes that controls the diameter of the pupils, the smooth muscles of the GIT, the male prostate and the urinary sphincter. They act like adrenaline on those sites. So please predict the actions of those agents before clicking onto this next video to see if you are right.
