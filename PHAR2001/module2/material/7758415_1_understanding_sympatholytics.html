Introduction to sympatholytic drugs

Sympatholytic drugs are drugs that block the sympathetic nervous system. Other names for the drugs include sympathetic nervous system antagonists, adrenergic antagonists, adrenergic blocking agents, there are many selective agents like alpha-blockers and beta-blockers. These are NOT the same as a parasympathomimetic agent but they do allow the parasympathetic rest and digest system to DOMINATE. So, predict what would happen with a sympatholytic drug .
