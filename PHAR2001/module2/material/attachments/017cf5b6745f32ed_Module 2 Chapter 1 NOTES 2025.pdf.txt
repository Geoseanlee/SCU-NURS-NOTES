          ANS pharmacology has one
          simple rule

         Drugs that affect the autonomic nervous
         system have one of four actions
               1. They can increase the sympathetic system
                  response
               2. They can increase the parasympathetic
                  system response
               3. They can decrease the sympathetic system
                  response
               4. They can decrease the parasympathetic
                  system response




17




          In medicine, there are always
          exceptions to the rule…
         Drugs that affect the autonomic nervous system have one of four
         actions
              1. They can increase the sympathetic system response
              2. They can increase the parasympathetic system response
              3. They can decrease the sympathetic system response
              4. They can decrease the parasympathetic system response

         EXCEPT:
            • Alpha 2 receptors (sympathetic system)
            • Central nicotinic receptors
              (parasympathetic system)
          LET’S IGNORE THE EXCEPTIONS UNTIL LATER
          (postpone readings until the appropriate time)
