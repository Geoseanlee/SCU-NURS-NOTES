## ppt/slides/slide17.xml
ANS pharmacology has one simple rule
Drugs that affect the autonomic nervous system have one of four actions
They can increase the sympathetic system response
They can increase the parasympathetic system response
They can decrease the sympathetic system response
They can decrease the parasympathetic system response

## ppt/slides/slide18.xml
In medicine, there are always exceptions to the rule…
Drugs that affect the autonomic nervous system have one of four actions
They can increase the sympathetic system response
They can increase the parasympathetic system response
They can decrease the sympathetic system response
They can decrease the parasympathetic system response
EXCEPT:
Alpha 2 receptors (sympathetic system)
Central nicotinic receptors (parasympathetic system) 
LET’S IGNORE THE EXCEPTIONS UNTIL LATER (postpone readings until the appropriate time)
