    Non-selective sympatholytic
    drugs
    Mostly used to treat hypertension
    Labetalol (know this drug; used to treat pre-eclampsia)

    Adverse effects
       • Postural hypotension
       • Blurred vision
       • Bronchospasm (dyspnoea; contraindicated in
         asthma)
       • Swollen ankles
       • Nasal congestion

    Alpha antagonists

    Recall the location of receptors:
       • Blood vessels
       • Smooth muscles of the GIT
       • Radial muscles of the eye
       • Sphincters of the bladder

    If you block the adrenergic activity of these,
        • Vasodilation = decreased blood pressure
        • Increased GI motility and secretions
        • Pupil constriction
        • Increased micturition

     Beta antagonists (…olol drugs)
     Can be non-selective:
        • Propranolol (know this drug; first of this class,
          but crosses blood brain barrier)
        • Timolol (know this drug; treatment of choice in
          the patient with hypertension and angina)
        • Acebutolol… (other examples)

     Or selective for Beta 1 or Beta 2 receptors, but
     there are no clinical applications for Beta 2
     antagonists
     Selective Beta 1 antagonists were developed to
     reduce bronchospasm
        • Contraindicated for those with asthma, COPD,
          allergies



16




     Beta 1 antagonists
     The blockage of the beta 1 receptors in the heart
        • Decreased chronotropic effects
        • Decreased inotropic effects
        • Together, these have the effect of
           • stabilising the rhythm and
           • leading to decrease in arrhythmias,
           • decreased cardiac workload
           • decreased oxygen consumption
     The blockage of the beta 1 receptors in the
     kidneys
        • Renin- angiotensin-aldosterone mechanism
          decreased
        • Decreased blood pressure


17




     Beta 1 antagonists
     Metoprolol (lopressor; know this drug)
     Betaxolol, Atenolol

     Adverse reactions:
        • Dizziness
        • Bradycardia
        • Orthostatic hypotension
        • Bronchospasm
     Never stop this drug abruptly!
