Understanding receptors of the ANS

The concept of subtypes of the alpha1, 2, 3, and 4-adrenoceptor was first suggested in the mid 1980s and since then we have started to develop more and more selective medications. Yes, it made our work as students a bit more difficult but it really decreased the side effects from such medications. Now, we can affect the heart without affecting the salivation. We can affect the peripheral blood vessels without affecting the lungs.

It takes a bit of practice but you will start to understand the side effects of a medication simply by knowing which receptors it binds to after engaging with this lesson.
