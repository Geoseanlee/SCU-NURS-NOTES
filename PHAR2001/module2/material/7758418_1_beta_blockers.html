Overview of beta blockers

Beta blockers had been the primary therapy for hypertension, but ACE inhibitors, angiotensin II receptor antagonists, calcium-channel blockers, and thiazide diuretics are generally preferred over beta blockers for the treatment of hypertension in the absence of co-morbidities. Nonetheless, beta blockers are still an important medication for hypertension, especially if it involves congestive heart failure , the type of heart failure that is chronic (long term) and can be treated for 20 years or longer.
