Introduction to non-selective sympathomimetics

In this lesson, we begin our discussion of the non-selective sympathomimetics . Long name - what does that mean? Well, a sympathomimetic means that it is a drug that acts like adrenaline. It mimics the sympathetic nervous system. 

Specifically in this lesson, we will get a good picture of what happens in anaphylactic shock, and why adrenaline is the main therapy and why antihistamines can also be of value.
