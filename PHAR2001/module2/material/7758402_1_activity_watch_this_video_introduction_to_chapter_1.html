Objectives, PowerPoint and Notes

The objectives for this chapter include:

  • Describe the general properties of the Autonomic nervous system (ANS) (including the bodily functions it helps regulate, its two neuron nature, the neurotransmitters used, and the enzymes that degrade those neurotransmitters in the synaptic cleft).
  • Give the other names of a sympathomimetic drug (for instance, adrenergic agonist), a sympatholytic drug (for instance, an adrenergic antagonist or blocker), an acetylcholine agonist (for instance, a muscarinic drug) and an acetylcholine antagonist (for instance, an antimuscarinic drug)
  • Name the structures affected by the alpha 1, the beta 1 and the beta 2 receptors of the sympathetic nervous system, and for each of those structures note the effect of a 1. Sympathomimetic drug, and 2. Sympatholytic drug.
  • Compare the actions of the sympathetic and parasympathetic nervous systems on the heart, the pupils of the eyes, the entire gastrointestinal system including the amount of saliva that is produced, the lungs, and the urinary bladder.
