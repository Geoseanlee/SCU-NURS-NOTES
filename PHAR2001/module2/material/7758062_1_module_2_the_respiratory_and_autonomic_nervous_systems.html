Module 2: The Respiratory and Autonomic Nervous Systems

Original Blackboard item · _7758062_1

The autonomic nervous system is the portion of the nervous system that regulates our most important involuntary physiologic processes. Module 2 allows you to picture the drugs that work by affecting the Autonomic Nervous System (ANS) and the drugs that treat disorders of the Respiratory System.
