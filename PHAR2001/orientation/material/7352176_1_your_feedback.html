How to provide feedback

Your feedback is incredibly important to us. Here are two ways you can share your feedback throughout the term:

Informal Feedback

For quick questions, suggestions, or immediate concerns, please feel free to reach out directly to your Unit Assessor or tutors via email or during workshops and tutorials. We're here to help.

Official Unit Surveys

Towards the end of the teaching period, provide confidential formal feedback via official university unit surveys. Your valuable input directly shapes the unit's future, so we'd love to hear from you.

You spoke, we listened

Your feedback helps us to make continuous improvements to our units. Here's how we have changed this unit for the better based on your insights:

You told us that…

...So we changed the exam to a 20% quiz in week 2 to make sure you have a good foundation with far less stress.


You told us that...
