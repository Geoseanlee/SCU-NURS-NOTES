Example questions

Example of a question from this Orientation module that may be seen in the week 2 quiz on Module 1:

A medication that has been determined by the TGA to need to be dispensed by a pharmacist but a prescription is not necessary is classified as a:

  1. Pregnancy category B medicine
  2. Schedule A medicine
  3. Schedule 2 medicine
  4. Schedule C medicine
  5. Schedule 3 medicine

Click below for the notes for the first three lessons and begin your folder of notes that will be invaluable in the week 2 quiz.

To maintain academic integrity in the quiz, all questions in the exam must be worded in a way that students must look up 5 different answers in order to find the correct answer for each question. Students will not have the time to do this for more than a couple questions, so a much better approach will be to make notes on the notes provided and refer to your notes during the quiz.
