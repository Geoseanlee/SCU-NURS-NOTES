Pregnancy Category A drugs are the safest medication during pregnancy. If taken as directed, they should have no adverse effect on the growing foetus

Pregnancy Category B drugs are a bit safer during pregnancy than Pregnancy Category C drugs and can cause more problems to the growing foetus than Pregnancy Category A drugs

Pregnancy Category D Drugs cause harm to the growing foetus, although that harm does not include teratogenic changes. Teratogens are substances that cause congenital disorders in a developing embryo or foetus.

Pregnancy Category X drugs are teratogenic and should not be given to a pregnant woman or even a woman of child bearing years.
