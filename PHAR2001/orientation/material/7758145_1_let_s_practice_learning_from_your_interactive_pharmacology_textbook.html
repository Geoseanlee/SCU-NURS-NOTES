Please engage in the following lessons

Click below for the notes for the first three lessons and begin your folder of notes that will be invaluable in the final exam.

To maintain academic integrity in the exam given that the final exam is online, all questions in the exam must be worded in a way that students must look up 5 different answers in order to find the correct answer for each question. Students will not have the time to do this for more than a couple questions, so a much better approach will be to make notes on the notes provided and refer to your notes during the exam.
