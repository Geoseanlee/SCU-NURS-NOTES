[5.40] Welcome to the new MCW. A simpler, more consistent way to access your learning at Southern Cross University during 2026.
[13.72] All units will move to this enhanced learning platform. Let's take a quick tour of what's new.
[21.00] To access your learning sites. Log in to the MCW homepage from the menu on the left.
[27.08] Select Learning sites, then select the tile to open the unit at the top of the page.
[35.28] You will see the site navigation bar.
[38.96] Use this menu to navigate to different areas of the learning site, including the learning content, announcements, discussion board, and gradebook.
[48.64] We'll look at each of these pages in more detail shortly. For now, let's explore the content page.
[55.08] The content page is your home base. On this page, you'll find the learning content, which includes a unit orientation,
[63.00] assessment information, and access to the weekly learning modules and readings.
[68.64] It's important to view the unit orientation before week one, as it contains a welcome message from the unit assessor.
[74.64] Your instructor's contact details and a list of the required resources.
[80.19] Beneath the unit orientation is the assessments folder. This folder contains everything you need to complete your assignments,
[86.27] including assessment briefs, submission portals, Gen II use guidelines, and more.
[96.43] In the class folder,
[97.83] you'll find your timetable as well as links to join your classes online and watch class recordings for units with required readings are.
[106.19] My readings link will appear below the class folder.
[110.39] Next you'll find the weekly learning modules which contain articles, videos and activities for self access study.
[117.99] Click on a module to expand. Then select an item to open it from here.
[123.91] Use the next and previous arrows to navigate between items. Another way to move through a module is by using the module navigation panel.
[134.79] Inside the module navigation panel you will notice indicator buttons beside each item.
[140.15] This is your progress tracker. An empty circle means that the item has not yet been opened.
[145.71] A half filled circle means that the item has been viewed, but not yet completed.
[150.73] To mark the item as complete, click the button and a green tick will appear.
[155.93] You can also track your overall progress from the module main page.
[160.85] Next, let's look at announcements.
[163.17] Your teachers will use announcements to send you notifications about things such as timetable changes and upcoming assessments.
[169.53] You'll sometimes get an email too. When there are new announcements. They will appear as a pop up when accessing the learning site.
[177.17] As part of your studies, your teachers might ask you to take part in online discussions.
[182.05] Select the discussion tab in the navigation bar to view a list of all forums for the unit.
[188.33] The gradebook is where you will find your assessment results, feedback, and grades when they are released by the unit assessor.
[197.45] Some units may ask you to participate in group work if this is the case.
[201.73] You can join a group and message your team-mates from the groups page. That's it.
[207.37] You are now ready to begin exploring your Maya Q learning sites.
[211.25] Remember, if you need help, open the help and support link on your Mizzou home page or visit the Student Hub.
