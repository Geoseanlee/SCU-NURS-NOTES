Omeprazole is enteric coated which makes the drug last longer in the system

Simplistically speaking, Omeprazole effectively stops the energy supply to the hydrogen potassium pump, while Famotidine is just a competitive antagonist, simply competing with Histamine 2, which is needed for the hydrogen-potassium pump to pump.

Omeprazole is an irreversible inhibitor of the Hydrogen potassium ATPase pump, and since that is such an elaborate protein/pump, it takes quite a while for the parietal cells to either degrade the drug in the parietal cell, or to produce more of that enzymatic pump.
