Untitled Multiple Choice

Most drugs are metabolised using First order kinetics, meaning that in the first half life, half of the drug is excreted. Then, the timer starts again, and half of the remaining dose is excreted in the second half life. After 4.5 half lifes, about 5% of the drug remains.

Ethanol (the alcohol that is in wine and beer) is metabolised by zero order kinetics meaning that it is initially metabolised in a linear way, with about one standard drink metabolised per hour. But after it has been metabolised to the point that there is only about < 1 standard drink, it is metabolised by first order kinetics.
