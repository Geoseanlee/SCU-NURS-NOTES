true

One of the main reasons that an enzymatic inhibitor has side effects is that the enzyme that needs to be affected to exert therapeutic effects may have other roles in the body which we do not want to affect. For instance, NSAIDs and aspirin inhibit the enzyme COX, which gives those medications the ability to reduce inflammation. But COX has protective roles throughout the body, and those are affected as one takes NSAIDs and affecting THOSE result in the side effects.
