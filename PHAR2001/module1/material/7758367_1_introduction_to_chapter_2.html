Introduction to Pharmacodynamics

Now that we understand some of the foundational information about medications, we can move on to the more complicated topics of Pharmacology, namely Pharmacodynamics. Pharmacodynamics basically means 'what the drug does to the body' - how does it work? 

Most of our medications work on cellular receptors, so it is important that in this chapter, we get a good, clear picture of what the cellular receptors are and how our medications can use those to exert their pharmacological effects.
