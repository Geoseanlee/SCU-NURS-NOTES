Competitive inhibition is a type of reversible enzymatic inhibition in which the inhibitor is competing with the substrate. When the medication is fully metabolised, there should be no further effects

Non-competitive inhibition is a type of reversible enzymatic inhibition that involves the drug binding to the enzyme and changing the shape of the enzyme so that the natural substrate does not bind.

Irreversible enzymatic inhibition destroys the ability of the cell to produce that enzyme
