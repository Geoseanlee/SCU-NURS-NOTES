When the cellular receptor is bound by the natural ligand, or a drug that mimics the natural ligand, that is the signal for the cell to do something.

The cellular receptors that most of our drugs bind to in order to perform their therapeutic action are involved in communication between other cells or other parts of the body

Cellular receptors can be increased or decreased by the cell, thereby increasing or decreasing the number of messages that to to the cell (provided there are adequate ligands to bind to those receptors).

Although there are slight variations in cellular receptors, they are all quite similar.
