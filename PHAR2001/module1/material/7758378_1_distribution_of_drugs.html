How drugs are distributed within the body

Untitled Multiple Choice

Lipophilic (oil based) drugs often access a protein carrier molecule so that they can be carried through the circulation.

Each drug is different as to the % of the drug is bound to a carrier protein. Sometimes it is a high protein binding (for instance, 99%) and other times it is a low protein binding (for instance, 10%)

Only the unbound portion of the drug (the drug that is not bound to the proteins) is active and it is only the unbound portion of the drug that can be metabolised.

The % of the drug that is bound to proteins will always remain bound to the proteins until it is degraded in the body

Please choose the statement(s) that is/are correct.
