Introduction

Normally, medications work on cellular receptors or by affecting enzymes. They go through the process of pharmacokinetics, which involves absorption, distribution, metabolism and excretion. So, understanding them is pretty difficult. In this topic/chapter, we are going to start out with the easiest medications of the digestive system, the antacids and the laxatives. Since these do not get absorbed and they work by chemical or direct action rather on cellular receptors or enzymes, they are in themselves, relatively easy to understand. But remember that as you go through these topics, you are learning the foundation for your understanding of all medications, so please follow the step-by-step process of going through all of the material presented here. 

In this module, the only NOTES that are available are notes: three to a page with spaces for your own notes. In subsequent modules, PowerPoints and notes will be available. 

Click directly below to access the notes, which are arranged in order of the way the material is presented, from the introductory video lesson to the summary lesson.
