The importance of stomach acid

Histamine 2 blockers (the drug class with the ...tidine suffix) do not block all of the acid from the stomach like proton pump inhibitors (the ...prazole drugs) do, but nonetheless, they have similar long-term side effects associated with the greatly reduced stomach acid.

If you think about it, the acid is there for a reason. And some clients will be blocking that acid production for years. So let's quickly review the functions of the acid in the stomach and see how that ties into the side effects that are likely from long-term decreases in stomach acid. 

Video: Understanding side effects of reduced stomach acid

Millions of Australians use these medications that are available without a prescription and without knowing the potential side effects associated with their long term use. Understand those effects in just 5 minutes with this video, and you will be able to help clients or friends who decrease their GIT acidity without getting the problem properly assessed.
