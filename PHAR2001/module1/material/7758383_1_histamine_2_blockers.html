Safety issue

The most popular Histamine 2 receptor blockers (due to better efficacy and potency) was Ranitidine (Zantac) until 2020 when it was discovered that some components of the drug broke down into carcinogenic components. This happened to a greater extent over time and if the drug was stored improperly, like in a hot car. The company that manufactured Zantac has changed the drug from Ranitidine to Famotidine and changed the brand name of their version of Famotidine to Zantac 360. Clients who mention having Zantac should be questioned to make sure they are not taking leftover Ranitidine. 

Video: Histamine 2  receptor blockers

All other Histamine 2 blockers like Famotidine and Cimetidine and other ...tidine drugs have been tested for carcinogens and none were found. This video excludes mention of Ranitidine and attempts to give you a good picture of the other members of the Drug Class called the Histamine 2 receptor blocker drugs.

Histamine 2 receptor antagonists work on a specific cell called the parietal cell that is located in gastric pits of the lining of the stomach.

Histamine 2 receptor antagonists affect the parietal cell directly, without having to be absorbed, because the medication is exposed to those cells when it is in the stomach.

The parietal cells have a trans-membrane protein carrier called a hydrogen potassium ATPase pump that secrete the H+ (sometimes called protons; sometimes called ‘acid’) into the gastric pits and on to the stomach, but need three things to bind to the cell at the same time in order for that to occur, Histamine 2, Acetylcholine (from the parasympathetic rest and digest system) and gastrin (Gastrin is a hormone that is released when there are peptides in the stomach.).

The Histamine 2 receptor blockers like Famotidine and other ….tidine drugs are competitive antagonists, so they compete with the Histamine 2 that is in the area of the parietal cell. There is still some Histamine 2 binding in the parietal cell and still some acid being secreted, so it will never be 100% effective,
