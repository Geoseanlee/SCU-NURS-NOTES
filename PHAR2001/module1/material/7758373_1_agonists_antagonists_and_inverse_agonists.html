It can act as an agonist, acting like the natural ligand, and performing the same physiological response as the natural ligand.

It can act as an antagonist, binding to the receptor and just blocking it, competing with the natural ligand and thereby decreasing the messages that are sent to the cell.

On certain receptors, it can act as an inverse agonist, which is simplistically referred to by many authors as an antagonist.
