Understanding constipation

Remember that we need to:

  • know the general physiology of the area and
  • aim to understand a bit about the disorder (the PATHO-physiology) that the medication we are studying is used for.

Before we can understand why our medications are being used, how they will work and in what circumstances they will be contraindicated. The alternative is to memorise long lists of drugs that we don't understand at all. While we are talking about constipation in this instance, which is relatively easy, we will apply these techniques to be able to understand much more difficult disorders and drugs. 

This section allows you to understand constipation so that you can safely recommend the best therapy.
