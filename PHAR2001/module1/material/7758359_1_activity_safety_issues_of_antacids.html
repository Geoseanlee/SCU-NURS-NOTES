The rebound hyperacidity of the antacids can contribute to the development of chronic problems like dysplastic changes in the oesophagus (called Barrett's disease or Barrett's Oesophagus)

The rebound hyperacidity may contribute to the development of a peptic ulcer

The client may not get proper medical advice, resulting in the advancement of dysplastic changes in the oesophagus

Antacids taken incorrectly can affect the absorption of some nutrients, resulting in nutritional deficiencies.
