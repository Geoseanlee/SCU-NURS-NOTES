Osmotic laxatives

Can you really picture the process of osmosis? If so, that is great, you will understand the osmotic laxatives quite easily. If not, this video will help you picture the process as you learn about the osmotic laxatives with this lesson. One of the most important points about osmotic laxatives is the fact that they develop a linear dose response. You will find out what this is and why it is very uncommon with our medications and very dangerous to assume that our other medications have that type of response. 

In the quiz at the end of the Term, I will definitely include a question about linear dose response and other material presented in this 6-minute video. Please watch it to learn more about osmosis, safety issues of osmotic laxatives, linear dose response curve, normal dose response of medications and even the safety issues of paracetamol. Osmotic laxatives include lactulose and magnesium citrate

The osmotic laxatives exhibit a LINEAR dose-response curve, meaning the more you take, the greater the effect.

The osmotic laxatives result in more fluid in the GIT, and that fluid comes from the blood (plasma)

Since the osmotic laxatives do not get absorbed and they simply work by direct action, there are no precautions needed when taking them.
