Introduction to neurotransmitters

This is the condensed version (assessable portions only) of the last chapter of Module 1, and deals with drugs that work to control emesis or vomiting. 

The aims of this chapter are to:

  • Identify the safety issues associated with vomiting
  • Explain the four pathways involved in emesis
  • Place our medications into the context of the above 

Please feel free to use the NOTES for the condensed Chapter 5 directly below. If you would like the full notes, please go to the full chapter 5.
