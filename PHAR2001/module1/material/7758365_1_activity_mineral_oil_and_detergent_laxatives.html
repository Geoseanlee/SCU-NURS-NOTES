true

The main safety concern about mineral oil and detergent laxatives like docusate is that they should never be used together, as that allows some of the mineral oil to be absorbed. Once in the body, there is no method of excretion, and the immune system will form granulomas in an attempt to 'hide' the mineral oil from the rest of the body.
