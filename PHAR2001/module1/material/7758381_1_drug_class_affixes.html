Drug name classifications

Have you ever looked at the label on a container of pills and your eyes twisted the name in all sorts of ways to get the generic name make sense? It never does. ...or does it? If you learn a simple trick contained in this lesson, you will be able to identify general properties of drugs you have never seen before.  

Video: Drug class affixes

You may be wondering how you can learn quite a bit about 22 of the 200 most prescribed medications in 6 minutes and 17 seconds. Well, watch the video and find out! Don't forget to engage in the drag and drop excecise because that will help reinforce your knowledge. 

BTW: Ranitodine was mentioned here but has been withdrawn from the Australian market. Other ...tidine mentioned are all available.
