What are antacids?

Right now, we are learning how to study pharmacology in the most productive way. If we develop the best approach to studying medicines using the easiest medications, we can apply that to other more difficult medications and disorders.

Antacids are indicated foar the occasional relief of heartburn and contraindicated as the heartburn (or gastroesophageal reflux) continues for an extended length of time (becomes chronic). In this lesson, we will understand why that is, because what we don't want to do is memorise long lists of what medications are indicated for and contraindicated in.

So please don't think that the topic is not valuable just because it is relatively easy!
