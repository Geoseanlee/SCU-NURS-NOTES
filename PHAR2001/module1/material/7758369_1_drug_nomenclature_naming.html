Naming conventions

There are many different names for the same medication, so do we need to learn them all? Are there any tricks that we can learn to help us remember the most important drugs?

While we will no doubt need to know the common brand names of many of our medications, it is the generic name that is most important in the first instance, because that is the one that is always on the label. There are affixes (usually suffixes) to start looking out for and this video attempts to get you in the habit of finding that suffix, and identifying the drug.

After you watch this video and do the associated activities, you will never look at a drug name the same way that you do now!
