Introduction to the laxatives

We continue with our exploration into the easiest of our medications with a look at the laxatives.

Laxatives are the number 1 most abused medications because they are OTC (over the counter) and clients often self-prescribe when they should just be consuming more water and eating more fibre. Interestingly, there are a number of safety issues about the use of laxatives, so in this portion of the topic/chapter, we will learn a lot about how to safely suggest a medication with relatively easy examples. Remember, we are not just learning about laxatives, as we will be applying the information that we learn to other medications, conditions and scenarios. 

Video: Introduction to laxatives

This lesson begins the second half of the chapter, and as such I present the aims and general approach. Please watch the short 2.5-minute video and follow it up by quizzing yourself below. Remember that I made notes, 3 to a page to help you (all the way at the top of module), which you may find useful to jot down important points as you go through the videos. Writing things down has a really strong ability to reinforce knowledge.
