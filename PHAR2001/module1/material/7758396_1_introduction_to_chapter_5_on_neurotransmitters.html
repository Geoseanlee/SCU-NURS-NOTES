Introduction to Neurotransmitters

You do not need to engage in this full chapter for PHAR2001. You may use the condensed chapter adjacent to the point at which you enter this file.

This is the last chapter of Module 1, and deals with drugs that work on neurons in general and specifically, work to control emesis or vomiting. 

The aims of this chapter are to:

  • Generally explain neurotransmission and the ways that our medications can work on neurons
  • Identify the safety issues associated with vomiting
  • Explain the four pathways involved in emesis
  • Place our medications into the context of the above 

Please feel free to use the NOTES for Chapter 5 directly below: 

Module 1 Chapter 5 NOTES 2025.pdf

Video: Watch this video introduction to the chapter

Bismuth subsalicylate is not sold in Australia at all, and as such, there will be no questions in the quiz regarding that medication. Please just aim to be aware of its indications and effects, and the fact that it is not available here, because it is available in just about every other country, and is heavily relied on by many people who have frequent stomach irritation.
