Module 1: Understanding Medications using the Gastrointestinal System

Original Blackboard item · _7758061_1

Module 1 lays down the foundation for your understanding of medications, your ability to use them safely and your ability to streamline your studies in Pharmacology. Using one of the easiest bodily systems, the gastrointestinal tract/system (GIT), we explore pharmacokinetics, pharmacodynamics and other foundational information. This is a very important module that is twice the size of the average module in this unit. The week 2 quiz on Friday of week 2 tests your knowledge of the module.
