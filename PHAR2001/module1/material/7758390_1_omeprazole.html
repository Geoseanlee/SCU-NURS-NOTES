Since proton pump inhibitors such as Omeprazole and other ...prazole drugs decrease stomach acid by nearly 100%, they are the first line therapy for people with Barrett's Disease and Peptic Ulcer Disease.

Barrett's disease is a condition where there is oesophageal dysplasia, and it may progress to cancer if there is continued insult involving acid spashing up on the cells.

Peptic ulcer disease can result in a clinical emergency because continued acid can erode into a blood vessel, resulting in bleeding into the stomach.

If a client has been on proton pump inhibitors like omeprazole for 5 or more years, the person should be withdrawn from the medication
