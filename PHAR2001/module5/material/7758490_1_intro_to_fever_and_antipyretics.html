NSAIDs and COX2 inhibitors

In this lesson, we begin our understanding of the specific types of painkillers, with two groups of medications that actually work at the site of the pain. As tissues are damaged, the immune system cells come to the rescue, releasing cytokines, prostaglandins and other ‘mediators of pain and inflammation’. Those are the target of the NSAIDs and COX2 inhibitors, which are discussed here.
