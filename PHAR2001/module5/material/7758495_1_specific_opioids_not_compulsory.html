Additional types of opioids

The material in this lesson will not be assessed in PHAR2001.  

We have 17 different types of opioid receptors in the body, and their prevalence at various points in the body vary with each receptor. Meanwhile, our opioid medications affect each of those receptors with varying amounts of affinity. And to make it even more complicated, some of the opioids are agonists at one receptor and antagonists at others. It would take an entire unit of study to get a really good picture of all of the opioids, but in this unit, we first need to understand that we use 10mg IV morphine as the reference point and compare all drugs to that. And then we will take just a few of the most highly prescribed opioids and compare them to morphine.
