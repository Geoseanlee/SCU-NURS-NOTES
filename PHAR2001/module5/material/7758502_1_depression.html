Introduction to Depression

We are going to shift our attention to depression right now. Depression is a very common disorder that is very much linked to stress, as stress seems to dysregulate the area of the brain associated with mood. The present hypothesis is that the person who is depressed normally has a (relative) decrease in the levels level of serotonin and/or noradrenaline in the areas of the brain associated with mood. Therefore, our antidepressants mostly concentrate on increasing one or both of those neurotransmitters. There are a few ways in which we can do that, as we will see in this next video.
