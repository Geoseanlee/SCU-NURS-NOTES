Introduction to SNRIs

While SSRIs are the most commonly prescribed group of antidepressants, there is a newer group of medications that works in a similar way to the SSRIs but affects both serotonin and noradrenaline. The medication is called an SNRI (Serotonin Noradrenaline Reuptake Inhibitor). The side effect profile is similar, but in addition to the antidepressant effects, the SNRIs may have an effect reducing chronic and neuropathic pain. There are also what we call ‘atypical antidepressants’ that tend to work at multiple sites.
