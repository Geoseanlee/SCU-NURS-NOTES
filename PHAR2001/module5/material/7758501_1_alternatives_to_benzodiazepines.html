The need for alternative medications

Because of the fact that the benzodiazepines are addictive, are often abused, and are associated with an increased risk of developing Alzheimer’s disease, we are always eager to see alternative treatments for anxiety. Buspirone is not a benzodiazepine. But it is ineffective for panic anxiety, as it is mostly approved for just generalised anxiety. Another interesting medication that is mainly used for children is one of the first-generation antihistamines called Hydroxyzine. Recall that the first-generation antihistamines (like diphenhydramine), broke into the BBB and resulted in drowsiness.
