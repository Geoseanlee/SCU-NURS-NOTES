Introduction to specific antipsychotics

The material in this lesson is not compulsory in PHAR2001.  

The antipsychotic medications can be divided into the typical (first generation) and the atypical (second generation) antipsychotics. While both of those groups of medications share the same therapeutic goal, namely to block the dopamine 2 in the brain, the typical antipsychotics block all dopamine pathways about equally while the atypical (second generation) antipsychotics are more selective for one pathway or another.
