Treating Parkinson's disease

Parkinson’s disease is a chronic, progressive degenerative disorder that at this stage has no universal cure. Unfortunately, our medications can give a higher quality of life to clients but will not reverse or stop the progression of the disease. There are several different treatments of Parkinson’s and this lesson concentrates on the two most established treatments.
