Predicting analgesic combinations by mechanism of action

Oftentimes, you will find that two medications combined will yield an additive effect where the client enjoys the benefits of both drugs. Normally that does not occur if the person takes two drugs that work by the same mechanism. With the information in this lesson, you will learn where our analgesics work so that you can more accurately predict which of the medications may yield additional benefits
