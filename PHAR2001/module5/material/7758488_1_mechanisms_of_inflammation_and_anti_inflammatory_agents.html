Understanding inflammation

Inflammation is an important contributor to the pain many clients experience because the process of inflammation sensitises the nerves and allows greater propagation of impulses that are ultimately perceived by the brain as pain. This lesson will help you picture the process of inflammation and the type of medications that are used to decrease inflammation.

Corticosteroids (glucocorticoids) are ‘steroidal’ anti-inflammatory agents. They are some of the strongest anti-inflammatory medications and as such are used in the case of extreme potential inflammation including in life threatening asthma, autoimmune disorders, post organ transplantation and others. They are much different from the anabolic steroids like testosterone. The corticosteroids are actually catabolic, meaning that they break down tissue. They are also much different from the non-steroidal anti-inflammatory agents NSAIDs like aspirin and ibuprofen.
