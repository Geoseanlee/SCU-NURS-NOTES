The use of SSRIs in managing depression

The SSRIs (selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors are the first line agents in the treatment of major depression. There are more SSRIs in the top 200 most prescribed medications than we will need to learn but you really need to remember the ones in the top 20 most prescribed, which are sertraline (Zoloft), Escitalopram (Lexapro) and Fluoxetine (Prozac). These medications block the re-uptake of the serotonin, leaving the serotonin in the synaptic cleft longer to perform its inhibitory action on the next neuron. Lets take a look at this very important group of medications.
