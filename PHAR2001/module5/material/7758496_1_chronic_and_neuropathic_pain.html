Treatment of chronic and neuropathic pain

It is interesting to note that the analgesics like opioids that are very effective at reducing extreme, acute pain are generally not effective once the pain becomes chronic. That type of pain has resulted in changes along the pain pathway that magnify the pain and make the pain worse, so it needs to be treated with different medications. This lesson will give you some more insight into the treatment of chronic and neuropathic pain.
