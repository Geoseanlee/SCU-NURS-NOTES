Module 5: Analgesics and Psychotropic Drugs

Original Blackboard item · _7758065_1

In this module, we explore analgesics and psychotropic drugs. The term 'psychotropic' pertains to one's mental state. The term 'analgesic' means pain killer. The way in which these drugs are related is that they both affect neurotransmitters (directly or indirectly).
