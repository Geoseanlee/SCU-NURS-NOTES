The use of opioids

Opium is the exudate that comes from the opium poppy plant and contains morphine and codeine. Up until the early 1960s there was a medication called paregoric that contained opium and was sold to reduce diarrhoea, reduce cough and decrease pain. But it was also used in many households to settle down the children before going to sleep! Times have changed, and we no longer use opium or use opioids when our young children are over-excited (or pretty much for any reason), but the opioids remain a very important group of medications for adults. Let’s learn more about their present use.
