                 Pharmacodynamics

        Imagine what the drug does to our body…
       • Does it work on cellular receptors?
       • Does it work on enzymes?

       Pharmacodynamics includes the mechanism
       of action
            But we are only concerned about an
          overview of the mechanism of action –
            sometimes called ‘mode of action’
