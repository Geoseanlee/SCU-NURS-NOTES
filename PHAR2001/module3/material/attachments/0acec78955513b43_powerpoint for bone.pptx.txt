Bone health: Key cells and hormones
The balance between osteoblast and osteoclast activity is important
Osteoclasts
Digests the bone 
Secrete cytokines that stimulate resorption
One small section of bone at a time
Osteoblasts rebuild the bone
Bone formation and breakdown involves participation of the hormones from the parathyroid gland and the hormone commonly referred to as vitamin D

PTH: The hormone of the parathyroid gland
Parathyroid Hormone
 (PTH)
Increased release in response to decreased serum calcium levels
Increases 
osteoclastic
 activity
Liberates free calcium
Decreases excretion of calcium by the kidneys
Calcitonin is released from the thyroid gland
Responds to high serum calcium
Inhibits 
osteoclastic
 activity and formation

## ppt/slides/slide14.xml
Parathyroid gland
 secretes PARATHYROID HORMONE (PTH)
(Stimulated by low levels of calcium)
Increases absorption of calcium in the intestines
Increases the synthesis of vitamin D
Releases calcium from bone
Decreases renal excretion of calcium

## ppt/slides/slide15.xml
Important bone disorders
Osteoporosis
Decreased bone mass and density
Osteomalacia
 and Rickets
Softening of the bones due to decreased mineralisation
Most commonly caused by inadequate Vitamin D
Osteonecrosis
Cellular death of bone cells due to inadequate blood supply
Paget’s disease
Abnormal enlargement and weakening of the bone

## ppt/slides/slide16.xml
Glucocorticoid induced bone disease
Leading cause of secondary & iatrogenic osteoporosis.
Lumbar spine & proximal femur.
2 phases:
6-12% loss in first year.
3% loss pa thereafter.
Rapid increase in risk of hip and vertebral fracture.
Fracture risk increases by 75% in first 3 months of treatment before a decline in BMD.
Osteonecrosis
 may be as high as 40% of patients
Osteonecrosis is death of bone due to decreased blood supply
NEJM 2011; 365:62-70

## ppt/slides/slide17.xml
Prevention of osteoporosis
Calcium and vitamin D supplements.
Reduce rate of bone loss.
Vitamin D 1000iu/day.
No calcium supplements after about 50 y/o due to increased risk of MI
Exercise, especially weight bearing exercise before the age of 30

Treatment of osteoporosis
First Line:
Oral Bisphosphonates 
Inhibit bone breakdown and resorption
Selective localisation and prolonged storage in bone
Increase BMI in the 
femoral neck
 and 
lumbar spine.
Osteonecrosis of the jaw.
