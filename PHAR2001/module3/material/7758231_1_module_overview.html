Module overview: The endocrine system

The endocrine system is essentially a communication system. It depends on feedback loops of the hormones released by internal glands directly into the circulatory system that regulate distant target organs. But what happens when that communication system breaks down? This module answers that question. 

This entire module contains a total of 70 minutes of video lessons plus activities that reinforce your knowledge and prepare you for the quiz.
