The hypothalamus secretes Growth hormone releasing hormone (GHRH) which goes by the hypothalamus pituitary portal venous system, acting on the pituitary to release Growth hormone (GH). GH is released into circulation and rising levels of GH act in a negative feedback on the hypothalamus to decrease the release of GHRH.

An example of positive feedback occurs around day 12 of the menstrual cycle, where rising levels of oestrogen act on the hypothalamus to increase gonadotropin releasing hormone (GRH)

Generally, hormones are degraded about as quickly as neurotransmitters.
