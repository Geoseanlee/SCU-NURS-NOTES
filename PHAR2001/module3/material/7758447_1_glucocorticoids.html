Cortisol stimulates gluconeogenesis, the formation of new glucose in the liver

Cortisol stimulates tissues to use free fatty acids for energy instead of glucose

Cortisol stimulates protein breakdown to use proteins instead of glucose for energy

Cortisol inhibits protein synthesis in most body cells, in an effort to use the proteins as energy rather than glucose

Cortisol inhibits hunger
