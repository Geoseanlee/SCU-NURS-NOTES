The highest amounts of oxytocin are released during labour, during the suckling associated with breast feeding and immediately after orgasm.

Oxytocin has a very strong emotional response, being responsible for the trust and bonding in the mother after birth, after suckling and after orgasm (in males and females)

Oxytocin has positive effects on cytokines of the immune system, ultimately improving the healing process

Oxytocin has a long half life.

Currently, the main uses of oxytocin are to promote labour and promote milk production, although it is being investigated for many disorders including alcohol addiction.

Oxytocin has superseded ergometrine as the first line therapy in the initiation of labour, and control of postpartum haemorrhage although combinations are still available.
