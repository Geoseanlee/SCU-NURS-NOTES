A brief look at antifungal agents

Fungi are nature's way of degrading things, so if you want your belongings to last longer, make sure that you clean off the mould and mildew!

Fungi are found all over, and we have an astounding number of fungi that live in the natural skin flora, but the bacterial toxins from the resident bacteria keep the fungi in check. When an infection does occur from fungi, the term is a mycosis. That is where our antifungal infections come in, and unfortunately, because of the fact that fungal cells are so close to human cells, there is potential toxicity with most antifungal agents. The main difference between human cells and the fungal cells is that human cells contain cholesterol in the cell membrane, while fungal cell membranes contain ergosterol. So the ergosterol is the target of most of our antifungal agents.
