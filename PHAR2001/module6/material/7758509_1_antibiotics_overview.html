In many cases, a physician will treat a bacterial infection by giving a broad-spectrum antibiotic that has been proven to be successful in that general type of infection. However, if there is a good possibility that the infection is being caused by resistant bacteria, a Microscopy, Culture and Sensitivity (MC&S) should be performed in a lab, and a narrow spectrum antibiotic may be prescribed based on the results of the MC&S.
