This module has only 43 minutes of compulsory lessons. You may choose to engage in just the compulsory activities (choose the first chapter below) or choose the entire chapter which includes examples of specific antibiotics, antifungal agents, antiviral agents and chemotherapeutic agents by choosing the second chapter below. The third chapter is an interesting artistic reflection of the unit which is not compulsory.
