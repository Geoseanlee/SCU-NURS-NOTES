Specific antibiotics continued

In this lesson, we broaden our understanding of ways in which medications can kill bacteria with antibiotics that inhibit DNA, block protein synthesis and inhibit metabolic processes.

For instance, Sulphonamides including sulphamethoxazole inhibits the ability of the bacteria to synthesise its folic acid that is needed to reproduce. Those medications are bacteriostatic, keeping the microorganisms in check while the immune system gains control.
