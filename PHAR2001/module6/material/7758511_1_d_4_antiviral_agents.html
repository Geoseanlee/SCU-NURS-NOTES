Antiviral agents

We knew very little about viruses until the HIV scare in the early 1980s but since then our understanding of viruses has been advancing comparatively quickly. As such, the first-line therapies for viral infections will change before you get to clinic. So the good news is that you do not need to know many antiviral medications at all. But you really do need to have a general knowledge of viruses and of the antiviral medications. The next two lessons attempt to give you that broad understanding.

Untitled True/False Question

true

One of the most important recommendations about antiviral medications is that they need to be administered almost as soon as any symptoms are felt. The reason for this is the exponential replication of the virus. Nearly every time viral particles are released from host cells, the host cell dies, leading to many of the symptoms. By the time we notice symptoms, there is mass destruction of cells already.
