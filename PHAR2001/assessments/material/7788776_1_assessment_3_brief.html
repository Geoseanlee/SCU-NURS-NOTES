Learning Outcomes
This 
assessment evaluates your achievement of the following Unit Learning Outcomes:
ULO 1: 
Explain the main principles of pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics
ULO 2. Describe 
the mechanism of action, pharmacological effects, therapeutic uses, contraindications, safety issues and side effects of major drug classes and commonly used therapeutic agents.
