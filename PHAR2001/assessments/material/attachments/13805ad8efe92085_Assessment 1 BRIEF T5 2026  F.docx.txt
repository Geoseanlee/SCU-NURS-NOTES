Learning Outcomes
You will demonstrate the following Unit Learning Outcomes on the successful completion of this task:
LO 1. Explain the main principles of pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics
LO 2. Describe the mechanism of action, pharmacological effects, therapeutic uses, contraindications, safety issues and side effects of major drug classes and commonly used therapeutic agents
Assessment Rationale
It is imperative that all health care workers have a solid understanding of pharmacology, especially the safety issues around specific medications and fatal drug interactions. This assessment attempts to ensure that your understanding of pharmacology is adequate for working safely in a health care environment.
