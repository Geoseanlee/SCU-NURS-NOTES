# NURS5010 Module 6 Notes
## Final Preparation, Consolidation and Clinical Skills Readiness

> This final module focuses on preparation for clinical skills assessment, final reflection,
> consolidation of the semester and readiness for safe, professional practice.

---

## 0. Module Overview

**Key topics:**
- final preparation for clinical skills assessment
- revision of core nursing skills
- reflective practice and self-evaluation
- readiness for placement and practice
- final consolidation of safety, communication and professionalism

**Nursing focus:**
- check competency before assessment
- identify gaps and seek feedback
- maintain professional standards and communication
- practise with safety and confidence

---

## 1. Clinical Skills Preparation

Before assessment, students should review essential nursing tasks, including but not limited to:
- vital signs
- infection prevention and hand hygiene
- patient positioning and mobility safety
- communication and handover
- documentation
- fluid balance and elimination care
- comfort and hygiene care

### Key principle
Assessment readiness is not only about memorising sequences, but about safe, thoughtful and
person-centred performance.

---

## 2. Reflection and Self-Assessment

Final reflection helps students identify strengths, learning gaps and future actions.

### Useful questions
- What skills am I most confident in?
- Where do I still need support or clarification?
- How do I know my practice is safe and patient-centred?
- What will I do differently in future clinical placement?

### Why it matters
Reflection supports professional growth and ongoing quality improvement.

---

## 3. Safety and Professional Readiness

Practising safely requires attention to:
- scope of practice
- infection prevention
- patient dignity and privacy
- communication and consent
- documentation quality
- escalating concerns appropriately
- acting with humility and accountability

---

## 4. Final Consolidation

This module brings together skills learned earlier in the semester and reinforces the idea that
nursing care is both technical and relational.

### The foundations are:
- person-centred care
- communication
- clinical reasoning
- infection prevention
- safety and accountability
- respect for patient dignity and autonomy

---

## 5. Key Takeaways

- Final preparation should be practical, honest and reflective.
- Competence develops through repeated rehearsal, feedback and correction.
- A safe nurse is both skilled and self-aware.
- Good nursing care is relational, ethical and evidence-based.

---

## 6. Final Reflection

This module encourages students to look ahead to clinical practice with confidence, humility and a
learning mindset. Safe nursing is built on readiness, teamwork and professionalism.

*Use your assessments, feedback and self-reflection to guide ongoing practice.*
