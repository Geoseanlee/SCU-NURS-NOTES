Information for Week 6
-
-
A brief overview of the key concepts and skills you’ll revisit this week as you consolidate your learning before assessment.
This module provides an opportunity to consolidate the knowledge and clinical skills you have developed throughout the unit. Rather than introducing new content, the focus is on reviewing and integrating key concepts to support safe and effective nursing practice.
You will revisit essential areas including infection prevention and control, vital signs, and documentation. You will also participate in a mini clinical skills activity designed to prepare you for the Week 7 clinical skills assessment.
Learning Objectives
At the completion of this module, you will be able to:
- Review and integrate key knowledge from previous modules
- Apply clinical skills in a simulated context
- Prepare effectively for the Week 7 clinical skills assessment
Activity: Clinical Skills Consolidation (Mini Assessment)
In this module, you will participate in a mini clinical skills activity designed to support your preparation for the Week 7 clinical skills assessment.
This activity provides an opportunity to:
- Apply the knowledge and skills you have developed throughout the unit
- Practice clinical techniques in a safe learning environment
- Identify areas where further revision may be required
Preparation
Before completing this activity, you are expected to review key content from previous modules, including:
- Infection prevention and control
- Vital signs assessment
- Documentation and charting
Active participation in this consolidation activity will support your confidence and readiness for the formal clinical skills assessment. This is your opportunity to bring everything together — focus on understanding the why behind each action, not just the steps involved.
This final module marks the transition from learning to application, supporting you to practice safely and confidently as you prepare for clinical assessment.


===== FILE: 04 - Preparing for your clinical skills assessment.epub =====
Preparing for your clinical skills assessment
-
-
Guidance and resources to help you understand the assessment structure and prepare confidently for Week 7.
This week is your opportunity to consolidate your learning and build confidence before the Week 7 clinical skills assessment. The strategies below will help you revise key content areas and practice the skills you will be assessed on.
Vital Signs
- Practice at home using simple tools (thermometer, watch with a second hand, or BP cuff if available)
- Review normal ranges, correct techniques, and common errors
- Use flashcards to memorise normal values and red flags
- Practice short scenarios (e.g., post‑operative patient, febrile patient, early deterioration)
Infection Prevention and Control
- Review hand hygiene and PPE sequences
- Practice cleaning shared equipment
- Understand standard vs transmission‑based precautions
- Reflect on how infection control protects both patients and staff
Documentation
- Practice writing clear, time‑stamped entries
- Use precise, measurable language
- Review examples provided in Blackboard or your textbook
- Link documentation to clinical reasoning and decision‑making
General Study Strategies
- Revisit self‑directed activities and complete any gaps
- Use Blackboard resources, including videos, charts, and practice materials
- Form study groups to practice skills and discuss scenarios
- Ask questions in labs and seek support early
- After each practice session, reflect: What went well? What needs improvement?

===== FILE: 05 - Final Reflection.epub =====
Final Reflection
-
-
Final reflection
Reflect on the knowledge and skills you have developed across the past five weeks. Consider how practitioner self‑care, communication (ISBAR), manual handling, mobility, vital signs, hydration and nutrition, fluid balance, documentation, personal hygiene, sleep and rest, and sensory impairment all connect to holistic nursing care.
How do these elements prepare you to recognise early signs of deterioration, communicate effectively within the healthcare team, and uphold patient dignity?
In your reflection, identify:
- one area of strength you feel confident in
- one area you aim to further develop before the clinical skills assessment
Use the template provided to guide your thinking.
Reflection template instructions
Part 1: Self‑Assessment
- Consider each skill separately.
- Ask yourself:
- How confident did I feel?
- What was one strength I showed?
- What is one area I’d like to improve?
- Write your responses in the spaces provided.
Part 2: Integration
- Reflect on how these skills connect in real patient care.
- For example:
- Did your fluid balance charting highlight something linked to vital signs?
- Did ISBAR help you communicate that clearly?
- Write one or two examples showing how your skills worked together.
Part 3: Reflection & Next Steps
- Identify which skill you feel more confident in after today.
- Decide what you will focus on practicing next time.
- Think ahead: How will these skills support you in clinical placement?
- You can complete your reflection using the Reflection Template.docx provided.
Reflection Template.docx
A Note on Reflection
Remember, reflection isn’t about perfection — it’s about progress. Every time you pause to reflect, you strengthen your professional identity as a nurse who is accountable, compassionate, and always learning. Take your time with this template and use it as a tool to guide your growth.
When you have completed your reflection, check your readiness for Week 7 using the Readiness Checklist in the Assessment and Submission folder. It’s a helpful way to identify what you feel confident in and what you may want to revise before your clinical skills assessment.

Summary

Throughout this consolidation module, we have revisited and integrated the core skills and concepts developed over the past five weeks. This is an opportunity to showcase not only your technical ability, but also your clinical reasoning, communication, and professional accountability.
Take the time to review key skills and practise where possible — confidence grows from preparation.
You are now well prepared to demonstrate safe, compassionate, and professional nursing care. The tutors, along with myself, wish you all the very best as you prepare for and undertake your Clinical Skills Assessment.
