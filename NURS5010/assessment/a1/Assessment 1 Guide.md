## What This Assessment Is

A **structured reflective portfolio** — 5 sections in the provided template. You reflect on a real, personal moment related to your wellbeing or limits as a developing nurse, then connect it to professional nursing standards.

**This is personal writing, not an essay.** Write in first person ("I felt...", "I noticed..."). No perfect story needed — genuine insight is what's marked.

---

## The 5 Sections

### Section 1 — Personal Reflection and Insight (~180–200 words)
**What:** Describe a specific moment where you became aware of your wellbeing, limits, or need for self-care — in a learning or caregiving context.

**How to approach it:**
- Pick **one concrete moment** (a simulation lab, a difficult conversation, a moment of exhaustion or overwhelm)
- Describe what happened, where you were, what you noticed in yourself
- Keep it focused — one moment, not a general feeling

**Example angle:** During a simulation lab you were asked to monitor vitals and communicate with a distressed patient simultaneously. You noticed your focus narrowing and felt your confidence drop. You realised you weren't sure how to prioritise.

---

### Section 2 — Reflective Insight / Understanding of Self-Care (~280–300 words)
**What:** Analyse what that moment taught you about yourself — your emotional responses, your developing professional identity.

**How to approach it:**
- Go deeper than just describing — *why* did you react that way?
- What does it say about your self-awareness, emotional regulation, or professional growth?
- Connect to ideas from Module 1: reflective practice, emotional intelligence, self-care as a safety issue

**Key concepts to draw on:**
- Reflective practice (Schön's reflection-on-action, SPROUT model)
- Emotional intelligence — recognising your own triggers
- Self-care as a professional skill, not a personal weakness

---

### Section 3 — Professional Standards Connection (~180–200 words)
**What:** Explain how your reflection links to NMBA documents — RN Standards for Practice, Code of Conduct, and/or Code of Ethics.

**How to approach it:**
- You must reference **at least 1** NMBA document (can use up to all 3)
- Cite informally in-text: e.g. *(NMBA, 2016)* or *"NMBA Standard 1"*
- Explain the *link* — don't just quote the standard, explain why it's relevant to your experience

**Useful standards to reference:**
- **Standard 1** — Thinks critically and analyses nursing practice (recognising your limits IS critical thinking)
- **Standard 3** — Maintains the capability for practice (self-care = maintaining capability)
- **Standard 6** — Provides safe, appropriate and responsive nursing care
- **Code of Conduct** — self-care as a professional obligation
- **ICN Code of Ethics** — nurse wellbeing as foundational to safe care

**References allowed (APA 7th, these 3 only):**
- NMBA Registered Nurse Standards for Practice (2016)
- NMBA Code of Conduct for Nurses
- ICN Code of Ethics for Nurses

---

### Section 4 — Evaluation of a Self-Care Strategy (~150–160 words)
**What:** Identify **one specific, realistic** self-care strategy. Explain:
- Why you chose it
- How it supports your wellbeing and emotional regulation
- How it helps you meet safe and ethical nursing practice expectations

**How to approach it:**
- Be specific — not "exercise more" or "sleep better"
- Choose something genuinely actionable for you as a student nurse

**Example strategies:**
- A 5-minute post-simulation debrief using the SPROUT model (describe the situation, what you felt, what you learned, how you'll apply it next time)
- A weekly 10-minute journalling practice after clinical content to process emotions before they accumulate
- Building a peer debrief habit with a classmate after labs — discussed in Module 1 (Debriefing and Mentorship)

---

### Section 5 — Professional Standards Innovation (~270–300 words)
**What:** Look outward — how should nursing standards *evolve*? Choose **one** contemporary priority:

- 🌍 Planetary health
- 🤝 Cultural safety
- 💻 Digital literacy
- 👥 Interprofessional practice

Then:
1. Identify a **specific NMBA RN Standard** (or part of one) where a gap or opportunity exists
2. Propose **one specific, realistic change** to strengthen it
3. Briefly explain: what the Standards currently do → where the gap is → how your change supports safe/ethical/modern practice

> ⚠️ You are NOT rewriting the Standards. You are showing you understand how professional standards evolve.

**Example angle (Cultural Safety):**
- Current: Standard 1 mentions critical thinking and cultural awareness broadly
- Gap: No explicit requirement for nurses to demonstrate cultural humility or engage with First Nations health frameworks
- Proposed change: Add a sub-criterion to Standard 1 requiring nurses to demonstrate culturally safe communication practices informed by Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander health principles

## SPROUT Model (Use This as Your Framework)

From Module 1 — this is the unit's preferred reflective model:

| Letter | Meaning | What to write |
|--------|---------|---------------|
| **S** | Situation | What happened? |
| **P** | Person | Who were you in that moment? |
| **R** | Reasoning | Why did you react/think/feel that way? |
| **O** | Outcome | What resulted? |
| **U** | Understanding | What did you learn? |
| **T** | Transfer | How will you apply this going forward? |

The 5 template sections map almost directly onto SPROUT.

## Suggested Workflow

1. **Choose your moment** — pick one real experience from your learning so far
2. **Free-write** for 10 minutes about what happened and how you felt — don't edit yet
3. **Map it to SPROUT** — work through each letter
4. **Draft section by section** — use the word ranges as targets
5. **Find your NMBA connection** — read the relevant standard, then explain the link in your own words
6. **Choose your Section 5 topic** — cultural safety is usually the most accessible for nursing students
7. **Grammar check** (AI permitted here)
8. **Format references** in APA 7th
9. **Write GenAI declaration**
10. **Submit draft** to practice Turnitin portal first, then submit final when ready
