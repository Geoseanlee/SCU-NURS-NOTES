# NURS5010 Module 4 Notes
## Skin Integrity, Bowel and Bladder Elimination

> This module covers skin integrity, pressure injury prevention, bowel assessment and care,
> urinary elimination, assessment and documentation. These are foundational skills for safe,
> comfortable and person-centred nursing practice.

---

**Key topics:**

- Waterlow risk assessment

## 1. Skin Integrity

Skin integrity is the condition of the skin and underlying tissues. Intact skin protects against
infection, fluid loss and injury.

### Risks to skin integrity
- pressure and shear
- moisture
- friction
- immobility
- age and poor nutrition
- incontinence
- chronic illness

### Nursing care
- provide regular repositioning
- monitor skin condition and moisture
- use pressure-relieving devices when needed
- maintain skin hygiene and hydration
- document skin observations accurately

---

## 2. Pressure Injury Prevention

Pressure injuries develop when pressure, friction and shear damage tissues over time.

### Prevention strategies
- risk assessment using tools such as Waterlow
- repositioning and turning schedules
- pressure-relieving mattresses or cushions
- skin inspection at regular intervals
- nutrition and hydration support
- moisture management

### Key point
Pressure injury prevention is proactive care, not only treatment.

---

## 3. APAT and Intentional Hourly Rounding

APAT and intentional hourly rounding are systems designed to reduce risk and improve patient
comfort, safety and responsiveness.

### Goals
- identify patient needs early
- reduce falls, pressure injuries and unmet care needs
- improve communication and responsiveness
- support dignity and reassurance

---

## 4. Bowel Elimination

A healthy bowel eliminates waste and maintains comfort, fluid balance and abdominal function.

### Factors affecting bowel elimination
- diet and fluid intake
- mobility/physical activity
- medications
- stress, anxiety and routine changes
- illness and surgery
- bowel obstruction or constipation

### Assessment
- abdominal history
- pain and distension
- bowel sounds
- stool pattern and consistency
- hydration status

### Nursing role
- assess constipation, diarrhoea, impaction or obstruction
- support dignity and privacy during elimination care
- follow safe medication and procedure guidelines

---

## 5. Abdominal Assessment and Bowel Sounds

A basic abdominal assessment includes inspection, auscultation, palpation and percussion as
appropriate.

### Bowel sounds may be
- normal
- hyperactive
- hypoactive
- absent

### Clinical importance
Changes may indicate obstruction, ileus, diarrhoea, inflammation or other pathology.

---

## 6. Assisting with Bowel Elimination

This may include supporting the person to use a bedpan, commode or toilet, or administering a
suppository if within scope.

### Nursing considerations
- maintain dignity and privacy
- explain the procedure simply
- observe comfort, pain and symptoms
- document output and response
- escalate concerning findings

---

## 7. Urinary Elimination

The urinary system removes waste, balances fluids and supports homeostasis.

### Common urinary problems
- urinary retention
- incontinence
- urinary tract infection
- oliguria/anuria
- urinary frequency or urgency
- pain or dysuria

### Assessment includes
- voiding pattern and urine output
- abdominal/bladder distension
- discomfort, colour and odour of urine
- fluid intake and dehydration risk

---

## 8. Assisting with Urinary Elimination

Nursing practice may include helping a patient use a bedpan or urinal, or assisting with toileting.

### Safe approach
- maintain privacy and dignity
- assess mobility and fall risk
- explain the assistance needed
- observe for difficulty, pain, or inability to void
- record output and assistance given

---

## 9. Documentation and Safety

Elimination care must be documented clearly and in context.

### Document
- amount and characteristics of urine or stool
- assistance required
- patient response and discomfort
- any abnormalities or risks
- relevant actions and escalation

Good documentation supports teamwork, safety and legal accountability.

---

## 10. Key Takeaways

- Skin integrity is a safety and comfort issue.
- Bowel and bladder function are important indicators of overall health.
- Early assessment helps prevent complications and support dignity.
- Accurate, timely documentation is essential.
